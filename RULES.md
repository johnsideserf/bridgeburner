# Bridgeburner

*A two-player card game of building & sabotage.*
2 players · standard 52-card deck · ~15 minutes per round

> This is the plain-text version of `Bridgeburner_Rules.pdf` (generated by
> `make_rules.py`). Keep the two in sync when rules change.

## Setup

Shuffle the deck. Deal **7 cards** to each player. Flip **3 cards face-up** in
the middle: this shared pool is the **River** (always 3 face-up cards). The
rest becomes a face-down **draw pile**. If the draw pile ever runs out,
shuffle the discards to refill it.

## How to win

Build a **bridge**: a face-up row of **5 cards in ascending value** in front
of you. Suits don't matter and gaps are fine — each card just has to beat the
one before it. **Aces are low.**

First finished bridge wins the round; play best of three.

### Face cards are mortar

A Jack, Queen, or King at the end of your bridge is tough: burning it costs
your rival their entire turn (2 actions) instead of 1. But nothing builds past
a King — overreach with mortar too early and you'll have to Demolish your own
work to keep climbing.

## On your turn: spend 2 actions

Mix and match, or repeat the same action twice — whatever the cost allows.

| Action | Cost | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Draw** | 1 | Take the top card of the draw pile into your hand. |
| **Build** | 2 | Play a card from your hand onto the right end of your bridge. It must be **higher** than your current rightmost card. Slow, deliberate work — a full build takes your whole turn, so your rival always gets a chance to answer. |
| **Burn** | 1\* | Discard a hand card that matches the **color** (red/black) of your opponent's **rightmost bridge card and beats it**. Their card is destroyed — and washes into the River, replacing its oldest card. **Salvage:** the burned player immediately draws 1 card from the pile — every act of arson arms your rival. |
| **Ford** | 1 | Discard any card from your hand, take any one of the 3 River cards, then refill the River from the draw pile. A known card beats a blind draw. |
| **Flush** | 1 | Discard all 3 River cards and deal 3 fresh ones from the draw pile. Fish for what you need — but your rival sees the new cards too. |
| **Demolish** | 1 | Remove the rightmost card of **your own** bridge to the discard pile. Sometimes you tear down a low card to rebuild a taller ascent. |

\* Mortar resists the flames: burning a Jack, Queen, or King costs **2**
actions. Nothing beats a King, so a King can never be burned.

## Arsonist's advice

- Every burn spent is a card not built — attack when it costs them more than
  it costs you.
- Early arson is charity — every burn hands your rival a fresh card. Save the
  torch for spans four and five.
- Watch the colors: ending your bridge on a card whose color you've bled from
  the deck is armor.
- The River remembers. Burned cards resurface there — don't hand your rival
  their card back.
- Mortar buys time, not immunity. A face-card cap costs your rival a full turn
  to burn — build while they swing.

*First bridge of five wins · best of three rounds takes the match.*
