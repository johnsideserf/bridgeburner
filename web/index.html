How to play

Win by building a bridge of 5 cards in rising rank (suits don't matter, gaps are fine, aces low).

2 actions a turn. Draw (1). Build (2) a card higher than your last. Burn (1) their top card with a same-colour higher card — it washes into the River. Burning a J or Q costs 2; nothing burns a King. Ford (1): swap a hand card for a River card. Flush (1) the River. Demolish (1) your own top card.

The pile is the clock. No reshuffle. When it runs out, the round ends: longer bridge wins, then higher top card, then the next card down.

Equal turns. If the first player finishes, the second gets one last turn.
